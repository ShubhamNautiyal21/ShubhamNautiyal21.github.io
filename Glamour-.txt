1- The Crowd

Mira has developed a hate for people, She was standing at the nouth of the metro station near Dwarika Delhi, at eight in the morning, watching the crowrd break against the turnstiles and reassemnble on the other side,
A hundered strangers moving with the same tired urgency, the same practiced indifferent to each others elows and bags  and breath,

"So much of crowd" Ayesha said beside her, her childhood friend, not really bothered by the crowd, but just noting them moving all around.
Mira didn't answer, she watched a man shoulder past an old woman without looking at her, watching two schooldgirls laughing at something on the phone screen while the world rushed around them unnoticed.

It hadn't always been like this she used to love mornings like this one, the noise of it, the aliveness of it, but that was a different version of her life and she didn't like to think too long about the difference,


They pushed through the gates together, Ayesha talked about nothing in particular, but just a show she was watching her landlord problem and how AI is going to kill the Humanity,
trying to fill the silence that has left Mira alone for too long, tended to curdle into something neither of them wanted to sit with


2- The Office

Mira's office was on the 5th floor near posh area near the Modern Delhi, A glass building that trapped head like a greenhouse, She reached her office past 9AM, two minutes into the office and she was already tired,
Across the room, Priya was leaning over their boss's desk in a way that had nothing to do with the quterly report opened between them,"A hand on his shoulder, a laugh half a second too long and a tilt of the head that put her hair excalty where the light would catch it", Mira watched it without being surprised by it, months ago, what she hadn't stopped being, was tired of it,

Ten minutes later, their boss walked past Mira's desk and dropped two folders in front of her, a follow-up document that anyone with functioning eyes could see belonged to Priya's work, no hers," Priya needs to handle some other assigned tasks" he said, "you have got the bandwidth",

Mira didn't have the bandwidth, she barely had it, that was required for her to survive her own life and work, none left over for someone else's flirtation tax. She looked over and caught Priya smiling into her coffee cup from the corner of the break room, exhausted Mira just nodded at her boss and sighed.

"I hate my life", she said to Ayesha, barely holding her tears,
Ayesha reached over and squeezed her right arm gently ,"I know," she said, "It's okay, I will finish my work and then help you over the B*** work"

It was such a kind of a feeling for her, that bother of them knew it, and neither of them minded, because sometimes a kind lie/gesture is the only thing holding a broken and an overwhled person  on a Tuesday afternoon,


3- The ride home
The Metro home felt worse than the Metro to the office, not just because of the exhausted energy all around and the stink, but the fact that, the same journey back to home, held memories, memories that now haunt Mira.

She found her usual seat, and let her head rest against the window as the train pulled out of the station and the city blurred into smears of light.
All started here, Not the relationship ,that had started somewhere softer, a mutual friends birthday party, the cliche way to find your "soulmate", with a conversation about the dying Bollywood industry and how they both hated the upcoming movie, just from its trailer, and then how they catch the same Metro but never saw each other,
The metro, did not initiate their relationship, but had woven it for the last two years, two years where she used to fight him like a child, for the window seat, Two years of his hand finding her under the bad on his lap, so no one else would see. because he was shy like that, sweetly, stupidly shy for a man who otherwise walked through the world like he owned it.

She used to love the crowd back then, Used to love the crush of bodies at rush hour because it meant they had an excuse to stand closer to each other, his chest against her back, his mouth close enough to her ear that she could feel him breathing before she could even heard what he said, She used to think that this is what people meant by when they say "Home is a person", all of the noise, the smell all around the ordinary chaos of the city trying to get back home, all of it had been beautiful, because he was in it with her, as if all other faces were blurred and she can only see him, she loved him a lot, she loved all the things about him and with him.

They used to get back home together in the same metro, used to sit in the same two seats, like it was reserved for them by some silent agreement with the universe. He used to bring her coffee from the stand outside her building even though he had to backtrack ten minutes to get it, and he never mentioned that, like it was'nt a sacrifice at all, as it is something you do for someone you love.
